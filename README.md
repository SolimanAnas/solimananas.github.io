### 🚀 Executive Summary
Registered Nurse and Paramedic with over a decade of experience in emergency and pre-hospital care. I bridge the gap between clinical necessity and technical execution by developing lightweight, reliable digital tools for EMS professionals.

**I specialize in:**
* **Clinical Workflow Optimization:** Streamlining data entry and protocol access.
* **EMS-Friendly Tools:** Designing interfaces that work under pressure.
* **PWA Development:** Offline-first applications for zero-connectivity environments.
* **Protocol Translation:** Converting complex CPGs into interactive logic.
